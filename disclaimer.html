Important Information About Our Beverage Powder Products

About Our Products

At Nimbuji, we take pride in creating refreshing lemon sharbat powder with natural ingredients and care. Please read the following important information about our beverage products before placing your order.

Beverage Powder Products

Crafted with Care

All our lemon sharbat powder is lovingly prepared in small batches using food-grade ingredients in a hygienic production environment.

Natural Taste Variations

Due to the natural ingredients in our beverage powder, there may be slight variations in taste, color, and dissolving properties from batch to batch. This is completely normal and reflects the authentic natural quality. Results may vary by individual taste preferences.

Hygiene & FSSAI Standards

We maintain strict hygiene and cleanliness standards during production, packaging, and delivery to ensure food safety. Our products are made following FSSAI guidelines for food-grade beverage powders.

Ingredient Information

Important: Please read carefully if you have food allergies or sensitivities to beverage ingredients.

Natural Lemon

Our products contain natural lemon extracts and citric acid which may cause reactions in citrus-sensitive individuals.

Sugar Content

Contains sugar and natural sweeteners. Diabetic individuals should consult their doctor before consumption.

Food-Grade Additives

Contains food-grade preservatives and flavor enhancers as per FSSAI guidelines for beverage powders.

Artificial Colors

May contain food-grade artificial colors to enhance appearance. Safe for consumption as per food standards.

Cross-Contamination

Products are prepared in a facility that handles multiple food ingredients, so cross-contamination is possible.

Contact Us

If you have specific allergies or dietary restrictions, please contact us before ordering to discuss ingredients.

Health & Medical Disclaimer

Not a Medical Substitute

Our lemon sharbat powder is a beverage product and not a medical substitute. We do not make any claims about health benefits, medicinal properties, or therapeutic effects.

Consult Your Doctor

If you have diabetes, dietary restrictions, medical conditions, or are pregnant/nursing, please consult your healthcare provider before consuming our beverage products.

Personal Responsibility

Consumption of our beverage products is at your own discretion and risk. Results may vary by individual taste preferences. We are not responsible for any adverse reactions or health issues.

Food-Grade Ingredients

All ingredients used are food-grade and safe for consumption as per FSSAI standards. However, individual reactions may vary.

Storage & Usage Guidelines

Shelf Life

Our beverage powder has a specific shelf life. Use within the recommended time frame printed on the package for best taste and quality.

Storage

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and moisture. Keep the package tightly sealed after opening to maintain freshness.

Quality Check

Always check the powder for any signs of moisture, clumping, or unusual odor before use. Do not use if the powder looks, smells, or tastes unusual.

Preparation

Mix with clean, potable water only. Use recommended quantity for best taste. Consume prepared drink immediately for optimal freshness.
