Refund & Return Policy

Fair and transparent policy for our beverage powder products

Last updated: September 2025

Our Commitment to You

At Nimbuji, we are committed to providing you with fresh, high-quality lemon sharbat powder. This Refund & Return Policy explains our approach to handling refunds and returns for our beverage powder products in compliance with FSSAI guidelines.

Understanding Beverage Powder Products

Important Notice - FSSAI Compliance

All our lemon sharbat powder products are food-grade beverage powders prepared following FSSAI guidelines. Due to food safety regulations and hygiene standards, we have specific policies regarding returns and refunds for sealed powder packs only.

Returns Policy for Beverage Powder

Sealed Pack Return Policy

We accept returns only for sealed, unused beverage powder packs. This policy is in place for health and safety reasons, as per FSSAI guidelines for food-grade beverage products.

  • Only sealed, unopened beverage powder packs are eligible for return
  • Opened, used, or partially used powder packs cannot be returned for hygiene reasons
  • This applies to all lemon sharbat powder variants and beverage products
  • Returns must be reported within specified timeline with proof of purchase
  • This policy helps us maintain FSSAI compliance and highest food safety standards

When Refunds Are Available

We understand that sometimes issues may occur with beverage powder products. Refunds or replacements are available in the following situations:

Damaged Sealed Packs

If your sealed beverage powder pack arrives damaged due to packaging or delivery issues

Wrong Beverage Product

If you receive a different beverage powder variant than what you ordered

Quality Issues

If the sealed beverage powder pack has genuine quality issues upon delivery (moisture, clumping, etc.)

Refund Process & Timeline for Beverage Products

Report Within 2 Hours

You must report any issues with sealed beverage powder packs within 2 hours of delivery for us to process your refund or replacement request.

Our team will review your case within 1 hour and determine the best solution for you.

Resolution

We'll provide a replacement sealed pack or full refund based on the situation and FSSAI compliance requirements.

Refund Methods

  • Online Payment Orders: Refund processed to original payment method within 3-5 business days

Replacement Policy

Free Replacement for Sealed Packs

In case of damaged or wrong sealed beverage powder packs, we offer free replacement with fresh sealed packs delivered at no extra cost.

  • Replacement sealed packs are prepared fresh and delivered within 2-4 hours
  • No additional delivery charges for eligible replacement orders
  • You can choose between replacement or refund based on your preference
  • Replacement beverage powder packs come with the same FSSAI quality guarantee
  • All replacements maintain sealed pack integrity as per food safety standards

What We Need From You

To process your beverage powder refund or replacement request quickly, please provide:

  • Order Details: Your order number and contact information
  • Proof of Purchase: Receipt or order confirmation for the beverage powder
  • Photos: Clear photos of the sealed pack showing damage or issue

  • Description: Brief description of the issue with the beverage product
  • Timing: Report within 2 hours of delivery for fastest resolution
