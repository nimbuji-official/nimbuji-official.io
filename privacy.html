Privacy Policy

Your privacy and trust matter to us

Last updated: September 2025

Welcome to Nimbuji

At Nimbuji, we are committed to protecting your privacy and personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you order our refreshing lemon sharbat powder and beverage products.

Information We Collect

When you place an order with us, we collect the following information:

  • Personal Details: Your name and phone number for order processing
  • Delivery Information: Your complete address for beverage powder delivery
  • Order Details: Beverage products you order, quantities, and preferences
  • Payment Information: Secure payment details for online transactions (if applicable)
  • Communication: Messages you send us via WhatsApp or phone regarding beverage orders

We only collect information that is necessary to fulfill your orders and provide customer support.

How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • Order Processing: To prepare and deliver your beverage powder orders
  • Delivery Service: To ensure your beverage products reach you at the correct address
  • Payment Processing: To securely process online payments for beverage orders
  • Customer Support: To respond to your questions about our beverage products and resolve any issues
  • Order Updates: To keep you informed about your beverage order status and delivery
  • Quality Improvement: To improve our beverage products and services based on your feedback

Information Sharing

We do NOT share your personal information with third parties. Your data stays with us and is used only for serving you better.

The only exception would be if required by law or to protect our legal rights, but this is extremely rare for a small food business like ours.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information:

  • Your information is stored securely and accessed only by authorized personnel
  • We use secure communication channels (WhatsApp, phone calls)
  • Physical order records are kept in a secure location
  • We regularly review our security practices

Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as necessary:

  • Order information is kept for up to 1 year for customer service purposes
  • Contact details are retained while you remain an active customer
  • You can request deletion of your information at any time

Your Rights

As our valued customer, you have the right to:

  • Access: Ask what information we have about you
  • Correction: Request correction of any incorrect information
  • Deletion: Ask us to delete your personal information
  • Opt-out: Stop receiving promotional messages (though we rarely send them)

To exercise any of these rights, simply contact us via WhatsApp or phone.

Cookies and Website Data

Our website may use basic cookies to improve your browsing experience and understand customer preferences for beverage products. These are harmless and help us understand how visitors use our site. No personal information is collected through our website unless you contact us directly for beverage orders.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. For a small beverage business like ours, changes are rare and usually minor.
